I am a Reader (equivalent to a tenure-track assistant professor) at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research – Centre for Applicable Mathematics (TIFR-CAM) in Bangalore. My research lies at the interface of mathematical physics, combinatorics, geometry, and probability. I study random lattice models, integrable systems, and their underlying algebraic and geometric structures.

I received my BS from the [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in/) in 2015 and my PhD from Brown University in 2020, where I was advised by [Rick Kenyon](https://gauss.math.yale.edu/~rwk25/). I was a Postdoctoral Assistant Professor at the University of Michigan from 2020 to 2023 with [David Speyer](http://www-personal.umich.edu/~speyer/), a Hedrick Assistant Adjunct Professor at UCLA from 2023 to 2025 with [Pavel Galashin](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~galashin/), and a Postdoctoral Associate at MIT from 2025 to 2026 with [Alexei Borodin](https://math.mit.edu/directory/profile.html?pid=1222).
